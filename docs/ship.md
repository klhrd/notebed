# what i made
there are two bookmarklet, a notepad and a screenshot taker. i spent lots of time making the ui/ux better, which i am proud of. i originally wanted to make more then just 2 tools but sadly i had no time, i had to make them after the ship.

## notepad
a note pad with
- resizeable box
- light/dark theme
- automatically local storage (note, position, size)

## snapshot
pin a screenshot
- resizeable box
- light/dark theme
- automatically local storage (position, size)

## what i learnt
- how to make a bookmarklet
- what a shadow DOM is
- how to use html2canvas
